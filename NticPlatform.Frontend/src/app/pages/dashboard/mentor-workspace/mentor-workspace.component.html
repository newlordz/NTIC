NTIC Mentor Guidance & Expectations

As an accredited NTIC Ghana mentor, your role is to advise and inspire your squads throughout the tournament lifecycle:

  • Technical Guidance: Help teams refine system architecture, debugging strategies, and hardware/software prototypes.
  • Competition Integrity: Ensure all work submitted by students represents original problem-solving and adheres to NTIC ethics guidelines.
  • Milestone Reviews: Check in regularly with team captains to evaluate project progress against national bracket deadlines.
